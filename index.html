Everything stays on your device

Framewright runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to a server.

  • Your files never leave this tab. Frames, videos and results stay in your browser.
  • Your own computer does the encoding, so speed depends on your device, not on a server.
  • No account or sign-up. Only the page itself and its fonts are fetched from the internet.
  • Closing or reloading the tab clears everything.
